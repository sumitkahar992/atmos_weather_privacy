Privacy Policy for Atmos Weather

Effective Date: January 7, 2026

Summary: Atmos Weather uses advertising, analytics, and subscription services to provide and improve the app. We collect limited data through these third-party services as described below. Your location is only used to fetch weather data and is never stored or tracked by us.

1. Overview

Atmos Weather ("we," "our," or "the App") is a weather application that provides current weather information and forecasts. This Privacy Policy describes how the App handles information when you use our services, including our use of third-party services for advertising, analytics, and subscription management.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Location Data (Optional): If you grant location permission, we access your device's GPS to provide weather for your current location. This data is sent only to WeatherAPI.com to retrieve weather information and is not stored by us.
  • Search Queries: When you search for locations, your queries are sent to Mapbox for geocoding.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

Through our third-party service providers, the following information may be collected automatically:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Advertising ID: Your device's advertising identifier for personalized ads
  • Usage Data: App usage patterns, features used, session duration
  • Crash Data: Crash logs, error reports, and app state at the time of errors
  • Purchase Information: Subscription status and transaction IDs (managed by RevenueCat)

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • User account credentials (no registration required)
  • Location history or continuous tracking
  • Contacts, photos, or other device content

3. How the App Works

  • Current Location Weather: If you grant location permission, the app uses your device's GPS to fetch weather data from WeatherAPI.com. Location coordinates are transmitted only for this purpose.
  • Manual Location Search: Search queries are sent to Mapbox Search Box API to retrieve location suggestions and coordinates.
  • Local Storage: Your preferences, saved locations, and settings are stored locally on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Atmos Weather uses the following third-party services:

4.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

We display advertisements through Google AdMob to support the free version of the app.

Data collected by AdMob:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • IP address (for geographic targeting)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

Opt-out: You can opt out of personalized advertising by going to your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads and enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (Android) or "Limit Ad Tracking" (iOS).

Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy

4.2 Firebase Crashlytics (Crash Reporting)

We use Firebase Crashlytics to identify and fix app crashes and errors.

Data collected:

  • Crash logs and stack traces
  • Device state at crash time
  • Installation UUID (anonymous identifier)
  • App version and configuration

This data helps us improve app stability and is not used for advertising purposes.

Privacy Policy: Firebase Privacy

4.3 Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the app.

Data collected:

  • App usage events (screens viewed, features used)
  • Session duration and frequency
  • Device and platform information
  • Geographic region (country/region level)

This data is aggregated and anonymized to improve user experience.

Privacy Policy: Firebase Privacy

4.4 RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

We use RevenueCat to manage premium subscriptions and in-app purchases.

Data collected:

  • Anonymous device identifier
  • Subscription status and purchase history
  • Transaction IDs from app stores

This data is used solely for subscription management and entitlement verification.

Privacy Policy: RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4.5 WeatherAPI.com (Weather Data)

We use WeatherAPI.com to provide weather information.

Data transmitted: Location coordinates (latitude/longitude) to retrieve weather data.

Privacy Policy: WeatherAPI.com Privacy Policy

4.6 Mapbox (Location Search)

We use Mapbox Search Box API for location search functionality.

Data transmitted: Search queries for location suggestions.

Privacy Policy: Mapbox Privacy Policy

5. Data Storage

Data stored locally on your device includes:

  • Saved locations and preferences
  • App settings and theme choices
  • Backup files (if created by you)
  • Unlocked theme purchases

This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to our servers. You can delete it by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

6. Backup and Restore

Backup files are stored locally on your device and are under your complete control. If you export backups to cloud storage services, those services' privacy policies apply.

7. Advertising

Atmos Weather displays advertisements through Google AdMob. Free users see native ads within the app. Premium subscribers (Atmos+) enjoy an ad-free experience.

Your Choices:
  • Personalized Ads: Opt out via device Settings > Privacy > Ads
  • Remove Ads: Subscribe to Atmos+ for an ad-free experience

8. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand app usage and improve stability. This data is:

  • Anonymized and aggregated
  • Not used for advertising targeting
  • Not shared with third parties for their own purposes
  • Used solely to improve the app

9. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

Atmos Weather offers optional premium subscriptions ("Atmos+") that provide:

  • Ad-free experience
  • Unlimited saved locations
  • Premium themes and icon styles
  • Widget customization
  • Backup and restore features

Subscriptions are processed through Google Play and managed by RevenueCat. We do not store your payment information.

13. Your Rights and Control

  • Opt-out: Disable personalized ads in device settings
  • Restore: Restore purchases through the app's settings
