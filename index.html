Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026 (Version 3.0)

Atmos Weather (io.github.atmosweather) respects your privacy. The App is completely ad-free, requires no account registration, stores your saved locations and preferences locally on your device, and transmits only the data strictly required for weather lookups, location search, diagnostics/analytics, and optional subscriptions.

1. Information We Process

1.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Atmos Weather does not require account registration and does not maintain developer-hosted cloud user databases. Your saved locations, cached weather forecasts and air quality metrics (Room database), app preferences, notification schedules, widget configurations, and unlocked theme settings (Android DataStore) are stored locally on your device.

1.2 Information You Provide or Authorize

  • Location Data (Optional): If you grant runtime location permission, the App accesses your device's GPS/network location through Google Play Services (Fused Location Provider) to retrieve weather for your current location. Coordinates (latitude and longitude) are transmitted over HTTPS to WeatherAPI.com solely to fetch weather, air quality, and forecast data. We do not maintain a server-side location history or continuously track you for profiling.
  • Location Search Queries: When you search for a city or place, your search text and a session identifier are sent over HTTPS to the Mapbox Search Box API to retrieve autocomplete suggestions and geographic coordinates.
  • Manual Backup Files (Optional Atmos+ Feature): If you create or restore a backup in Settings, the App reads or writes a local JSON backup file only to the device folder or storage provider you explicitly select via Android's Storage Access Framework. If you choose to export a backup file to a third-party cloud drive (such as Google Drive), that third-party service's privacy policy applies.

1.3 No Third-Party Advertising or Advertising ID

No Third-Party Ads: As of version 2.1.0, Atmos Weather displays no third-party advertisements and does not bundle any advertising network SDK (such as Google AdMob). The App explicitly excludes advertising-identifier libraries and removes AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION permissions from the merged Android manifest.

1.4 Information We Do Not Collect

  • Account credentials, passwords, or developer-managed user profiles (no sign-in required)
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or personal files unrelated to a user-selected backup file
  • Payment card numbers or bank details (handled exclusively by Google Play)
  • Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) or behavioral ad-targeting profiles

Depending on the features you use, Atmos Weather integrates the following third-party services:

2. Permissions We Request

Atmos Weather requests only the Android permissions required for its features:

  • Approximate & Precise Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) — Optional: Resolves your current location to provide local weather conditions and forecasts.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — Required: Checks connectivity and fetches weather data, location search results, subscription verification, and diagnostic telemetry.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — Optional: Delivers weather alerts and scheduled daily weather notifications.
  • Alarms, Foreground Sync & Boot Receiver (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK) — Feature-dependent: Keeps scheduled weather notifications, background WorkManager refreshes, and home-screen widgets up to date, including after device restarts.
  • Home-Screen Widgets (UPDATE_APP_WIDGETS): Configures and updates installed home-screen weather widgets.
  • Google Play Billing & Install Referrer (com.android.vending.BILLING, BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE): Processes optional Atmos+ subscriptions and Google Play install attribution.

3. Diagnostics, Analytics & System Backup

We use Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to monitor app stability, diagnose crashes, and understand general feature usage. These services rely on pseudonymous installation and app-instance identifiers rather than your name or email, and are not used for third-party advertising.

Android Auto Backup: The App enables standard Android backup configuration (allowBackup="true"). Depending on your device and Google Account backup settings, Android may back up local app data to your encrypted Google Drive backup.

4. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

Atmos Weather offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions (Atmos+ — Monthly, including a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers, and Yearly) that unlock premium capabilities such as unlimited saved locations, home-screen weather widgets and customization, premium color palettes and weather icon styles, and local backup/restore. Payments are processed directly by Google Play as merchant of record, and entitlement state is synchronized via RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

5. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

You can delete all locally stored locations, caches, and preferences at any time via Android Settings > Apps > Atmos Weather > Clear Data or by uninstalling the App.

Retention Periods:

  • Location Coordinates & Search Queries: Processed transiently to fulfill weather and geocoding requests; not stored in developer-controlled databases.
  • Local Weather Cache & Preferences: Retained on your device until updated, cleared, or uninstalled.
  • Crashlytics & Analytics Telemetry: Retained in accordance with Google/Firebase default retention schedules (typically 90 days for Crashlytics crash data and 2–14 months for Analytics event data).
  • Subscription & Purchase Records: Retained by RevenueCat and Google Play as necessary to maintain active entitlements and satisfy statutory tax, accounting, and audit obligations.

Deletion & Privacy Requests: Because Atmos Weather does not create or store user accounts, there is no user account to delete. If you wish to request deletion or access regarding any pseudonymous support or processor data, email sumitkahar073@gmail.com. Verified requests are processed promptly and within 30 days (subject to statutory tax or financial recordkeeping obligations retained by Google Play).

6. Data Security

Local data is protected by Android application sandboxing. All network communications with WeatherAPI.com, Mapbox, Google/Firebase, Google Play Billing, and RevenueCat are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Atmos Weather is not directed at children under 13 years of age (or the applicable digital age of consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App or by contacting us, please email sumitkahar073@gmail.com so we can promptly delete it.
